Common questions

How do I steer and jump?
Rest a finger anywhere on the screen and slide it left or right: the sleigh follows your finger. Tap to jump, or flick the same finger quickly upward. You can also keep one finger down to steer and tap with another to jump.
The steering feels too fast or too slow.
Open the map, tap the gear icon and move the Steering slider. Reset controls puts it back.
How do lives work?
You start every run with three hearts. Hitting an obstacle costs one and makes you briefly untouchable; the third hit ends the run. A shield or the turbo boost protects you without costing a heart.
How do I get three stars on a level?
Stars depend on the gifts you collect before the finish line. Tap a level on the map to see how many gifts two and three stars need. You can replay any level you have already unlocked.
How do I beat a boss?
Santa throws a present straight ahead every second or so. Line the sleigh up with the boss so the presents hit, and move off the red mark on the snow before his snowball lands.
What are gifts for?
Gifts collected in any run go into your sack when the run ends. Spend them in the Shop on longer magnet and turbo boosts, more frequent power-ups, a starting shield, and new sleighs.
The game asked to record my screen. Why?
That is for the Share replay button at the end of a run. You can decline; the game then shares a picture instead of a video. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.
I hear no sound.
The game follows your iPhone's silent switch, so check that Silent Mode is off and the volume is up. The music and effects sliders are under the gear icon on the map.
Will I keep my progress on a new phone?
Progress is stored on your device and is not tied to an account. It comes along if you restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one. Deleting the app erases it.
How do I start over?
Gear icon on the map, then Reset progress. This cannot be undone.
