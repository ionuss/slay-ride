Slay Ride

Privacy Policy

Slay Ride does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who we are

Slay Ride is an iPhone game published by Ionut Zamfir. If you have a question about this policy, write to zamfir.ionut@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not include any third-party tracking or advertising software. It does not connect to our servers, because we do not run any for the game.

What is saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • your progress: unlocked levels, stars, best score, missions and score multiplier;
  • the gifts you have collected, your upgrades and the sleighs you have unlocked;
  • your daily gift streak;
  • your settings: music and effects volume, vibration and steering sensitivity.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress inside the game, or by deleting the app.

Sharing a replay

If you allow it, the game keeps a short recording of your current run on your device so that you can share the last seconds when the run ends. The recording is made by iOS, contains only the game's own picture (no microphone, no camera), and is sent somewhere only when you press Share replay and choose where it goes. What happens to it afterwards is governed by the app or service you chose. You can decline the recording prompt and the game works the same; a still picture is shared instead.

Permissions

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

Children

Slay Ride is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Apple

When you download the game, Apple processes your App Store account and purchase information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that information. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may show us anonymous crash reports that we cannot link to you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes any of the above, for example by adding online leaderboards or sign-in, this page will be updated before that version is released and the date at the top will change.
